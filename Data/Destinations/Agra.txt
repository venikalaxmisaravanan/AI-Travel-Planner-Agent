City Name: Agra

State / Union Territory: Uttar Pradesh

Region of India: North India

---

Short Introduction

Agra is world-renowned as the home of the Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World and a UNESCO World Heritage Site built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan as a mausoleum for his wife Mumtaz Mahal. The city was a former Mughal capital and retains significant architectural heritage, including Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri nearby. It is unique for its concentration of Mughal-era monuments within a compact area. Agra suits history enthusiasts, architecture lovers, honeymooners, and first-time visitors to India, and is most commonly visited as part of the Golden Triangle circuit with Delhi and Jaipur.

---

Best Time to Visit

- Best months: October to March
- Weather: Winters (Nov-Feb) are cool (5-20°C), sometimes foggy; summers (Apr-Jun) are extremely hot (35-46°C); monsoon (Jul-Sep) brings humidity
- Festivals: Taj Mahotsav (Feb, cultural festival), Holi, Diwali

---

Recommended Trip Duration

- 1-2 Days

The main attractions (Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri) can be comfortably covered in one full day, with a second day allowing a relaxed pace or additional nearby sites.

Top Tourist Attractions

Taj Mahal
Description: Ivory-white marble mausoleum, UNESCO World Heritage Site, built 1632-1653
Time required: 2-3 hours
Entry fee: Rs 50 (Indians), Rs 1,100 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Sunrise

Agra Fort
Description: Red sandstone Mughal fort, UNESCO World Heritage Site
Time required: 2 hours
Entry fee: Rs 35 (Indians), Rs 550 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Morning

Fatehpur Sikri
Description: Abandoned Mughal city built by Akbar, UNESCO World Heritage Site (approx. 40 km from Agra)
Time required: 2-3 hours
Entry fee: Rs 35 (Indians), Rs 550 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Morning to early afternoon

Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)
Description: Marble tomb often considered a precursor in style to the Taj Mahal
Time required: 1 hour
Entry fee: Rs 20 (Indians), Rs 210 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Afternoon (soft light)

Mehtab Bagh
Description: Garden across the river offering sunset views of the Taj Mahal
Time required: 1 hour
Entry fee: Rs 20 (Indians), Rs 210 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Sunset

---

Hidden Gems

- Sikandra (Akbar's Tomb): Grand Mughal tomb often less crowded than the Taj or Agra Fort
- Chini Ka Rauza: A Persian-tiled tomb, rarely visited but architecturally distinctive
- Kinari Bazaar: Local market for an authentic, non-touristy shopping experience
- Guru Ka Tal: A historic Sikh gurudwara with a large stepped tank, quiet and lesser-known

---

Local Food to Try

Petha
Description: Translucent sweet made from ash gourd, a signature Agra sweet
Recommended Area: Panchhi Petha Store, Sadar Bazaar

Mughlai Cuisine (Kebabs, Biryani)
Description: Rich, spiced dishes reflecting Mughal culinary heritage
Recommended Area: Sadar Bazaar restaurants

Bedai and Jalebi
Description: Spiced fried bread served with potato curry, paired with sweet jalebi for breakfast
Recommended Area: Deviram Sweets, local breakfast stalls

---

Shopping

Popular markets: Sadar Bazaar, Kinari Bazaar, Subhash Bazaar
What to buy: Marble handicrafts (inlay work resembling Taj Mahal designs), leather goods, petha sweets, carpets
Approximate price range: Rs 100-5,000+ depending on marble/handicraft item
Bargaining tips: Bargaining is common, especially for marble handicrafts and souvenirs; prices can often be negotiated down significantly from the initial quote

Transportation

How to reach:
Nearest airport: Agra Airport (limited flights); Delhi's IGI Airport is a common alternative with onward road/rail travel
Railway station: Agra Cantt Railway Station (well connected, including high-speed Gatimaan Express from Delhi)
Bus connectivity: Good connectivity to Delhi, Jaipur, and other North Indian cities via highway buses

Local transport:
Metro availability: No
Taxi: Available, including app-based options
Auto: Common for short distances
Bike rentals: Limited availability
Public buses: Local buses operate but are less commonly used by tourists

Festivals and Cultural Experiences

- Taj Mahotsav (Feb): 10-day cultural festival showcasing crafts, cuisine, and performances near the Taj Mahal
- Holi and Diwali: Celebrated widely across the city
- Mughal-era heritage walks organized by some local tour operators

---

Safety Tips

- Areas to avoid: Poorly lit streets late at night, especially away from the main tourist zones
- Night travel advice: Use registered taxis or hotel-arranged transport after dark
- Emergency numbers: Police: 100, Ambulance: 102, Tourist Helpline: 1363
- General safety recommendations: Be firm with touts near the Taj Mahal; keep belongings secure in crowded areas

---

Common Tourist Scams

- Fake marble/handicraft shops with inflated prices: Shop at government emporiums or verified stores for fair pricing
- Unofficial guides overcharging: Hire guides only through official Taj Mahal guide counters
- Auto/taxi drivers pushing commission-based shops: Politely decline and stick to planned itinerary
- Overpriced "skip the line" ticket sellers: Buy tickets only from official counters or the ASI website

---

Travel Tips

- Best season: October to March
- What to pack: Comfortable walking shoes (extensive walking at monuments), modest clothing, sun protection in summer
- Local etiquette: Modest dress recommended at monuments; photography restrictions apply inside some structures (e.g., the main mausoleum at Taj Mahal)
- Language: Hindi and English widely spoken
- Cash vs UPI: UPI accepted in most shops; cash useful for smaller vendors and entry fees
- Network availability: Good 4G coverage in the city
- Medical facilities: Several hospitals available, though less extensive than in Delhi; serious cases sometimes referred to Delhi

Interesting Facts

1. The Taj Mahal took approximately 22 years to complete and involved an estimated 20,000 artisans.
2. The Taj Mahal's marble changes color throughout the day - appearing pinkish at dawn, white during the day, and golden under moonlight.
3. Agra served as the capital of the Mughal Empire under several emperors before Delhi.
4. Fatehpur Sikri was abandoned shortly after its construction, reportedly due to water shortages.
5. The Taj Mahal is perfectly symmetrical, except for Shah Jahan's own tomb, which was added off-center next to Mumtaz Mahal's.
6. Agra's petha sweet-making tradition dates back several centuries.
7. Agra Fort was the primary residence of Mughal emperors until 1638, before the capital shifted to Delhi.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is 1 day enough for Agra? Yes, for the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, though 2 days allows a more relaxed pace including Fatehpur Sikri.
2. Is Agra safe for solo travellers? Yes, though persistent touts near monuments can be bothersome.
3. Which month is best to visit Agra? October to March, for cooler and more comfortable weather.
4. What food should I try in Agra? Petha, Mughlai kebabs, and bedai-jalebi breakfast.
5. What should I avoid in Agra? Unofficial guides, unauthorized marble shops, and unregistered taxis.
6. Can I see the Taj Mahal at sunrise? Yes, and it is widely recommended for fewer crowds and better light.
7. Is the Taj Mahal closed on any day? Yes, it is closed on Fridays for non-worshippers.
8. Can I visit Agra as a day trip from Delhi? Yes, via the Gatimaan Express train (approx. 1.5-2 hours) or expressway.
9. Is photography allowed inside the Taj Mahal? Exterior photography is allowed; restrictions apply inside the main mausoleum.
10. Is tap water safe to drink in Agra? No, bottled or filtered water is recommended.
11. How far is Fatehpur Sikri from Agra? Approximately 40 km, about 1 hour by road.
12. Do I need to remove shoes at the Taj Mahal? Yes, or use provided shoe covers.
13. Is Agra part of the Golden Triangle? Yes, along with Delhi and Jaipur.
14. Are there entry restrictions on bags/items at the Taj Mahal? Yes, certain items like food, tripods, and large bags are restricted.
15. What is the best way to reach Agra? Train (Gatimaan/Shatabdi Express) is fastest and most convenient from Delhi.
