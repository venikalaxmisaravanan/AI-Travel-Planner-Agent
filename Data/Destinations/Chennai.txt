Short Introduction

Chennai, formerly known as Madras, is the capital of Tamil Nadu and a major cultural, historical, and economic hub of South India. The city is known for its Dravidian temple architecture, colonial-era landmarks, classical Bharatanatyam dance and Carnatic music traditions, and one of the longest urban beaches in the world, Marina Beach. Chennai is unique for blending traditional Tamil culture with a modern metropolitan economy centered on automotive, IT, and healthcare industries. It suits history and culture enthusiasts, food lovers, and travelers seeking a gateway to explore greater Tamil Nadu.

---

Best Time to Visit

- Best months: November to February
- Weather: Winters (Nov-Feb) are relatively cool and pleasant (20-30°C); summers (Mar-Jun) are very hot and humid (30-42°C); monsoon (Oct-Dec, northeast monsoon) brings significant rainfall
- Festivals: Margazhi Music and Dance Festival (Dec/Jan), Pongal (Jan), Tamil New Year (Apr)

---

Top Tourist Attractions

Kapaleeshwarar Temple
Description: Ancient Dravidian-style temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located in Mylapore
Time required: 1-1.5 hours
Entry fee: Free (camera fee may apply)
Best visiting time: Morning or evening

Marina Beach
Description: One of the longest urban beaches in the world, popular for walks and street food
Time required: 1.5-2 hours
Entry fee: Free
Best visiting time: Early morning or evening

Fort St. George
Description: First English fortress in India, built in 1644, now housing a museum and government buildings
Time required: 1.5 hours
Entry fee: Rs 15 (Indians), Rs 100 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Morning

Government Museum, Egmore
Description: One of India's oldest museums, with archaeology, art, and bronze gallery sections
Time required: 2 hours
Entry fee: Rs 15 (Indians), Rs 250 (foreigners), approximate
Best visiting time: Morning to afternoon

San Thome Basilica
Description: Roman Catholic basilica built over the tomb of St. Thomas the Apostle
Time required: 45-60 minutes
Entry fee: Free
Best visiting time: Morning

Hidden Gems

- Theosophical Society: A sprawling green campus with one of the oldest banyan trees in Asia, offering a peaceful retreat from the city
- Elliot's Beach (Besant Nagar): A quieter, more relaxed alternative to Marina Beach
- DakshinaChitra: A heritage village museum showcasing South Indian art, architecture, and crafts
- Vivekananda House: A lesser-visited memorial museum by the beach, tied to Swami Vivekananda's stay in the city

Local Food to Try

Idli and Sambar
Description: Steamed rice cakes served with lentil-based vegetable stew, a South Indian breakfast staple
Recommended Area: Ratna Cafe, Murugan Idli Shop

Chettinad Cuisine
Description: Spicy, aromatic dishes from the Chettinad region, including chicken and mutton preparations
Recommended Area: Restaurants specializing in Chettinad food across the city

Filter Coffee
Description: Traditional South Indian coffee made with a metal filter, served with milk and sugar
Recommended Area: Local "meals" restaurants and coffee shops citywide

Kothu Parotta
Description: Shredded flatbread stir-fried with vegetables, egg, or meat and spices
Recommended Area: Local street stalls and dhabas

---

Shopping

Popular markets: T Nagar (Ranganathan Street), Pondy Bazaar, Nalli Silks area
What to buy: Kanjeevaram silk sarees, bronze idols, spices, traditional South Indian jewelry
Approximate price range: Rs 200-10,000+ depending on item (especially silk sarees)
Bargaining tips: Bargaining is common in street markets like T Nagar; fixed-price stores for silk and jewelry generally do not allow bargaining

Transportation

How to reach:
Nearest airport: Chennai International Airport (MAA)
Railway station: Chennai Central and Chennai Egmore, well connected to major Indian cities
Bus connectivity: Extensive state and private bus network connecting to all major South Indian cities

Local transport:
Metro availability: Yes, Chennai Metro covers key parts of the city
Taxi: Widely available, including app-based (Ola, Uber)
Auto: Common; metered or negotiated
Bike rentals: Available in some areas
Public buses: Extensive local bus network (MTC) covering the city

- Tamil New Year (Apr): Celebrated with family gatherings and traditional customs

---

Safety Tips

- Areas to avoid: Isolated stretches of Marina Beach late at night
- Night travel advice: Use registered taxis or app-based cabs after dark
- Emergency numbers: Police: 100, Ambulance: 108, Tourist Helpline: 1363
- General safety recommendations: Be cautious of strong currents and undertow if swimming at beaches; keep valuables secure in crowded markets

---

Common Tourist Scams

- Overpriced silk saree sales claiming "factory outlet" status: Purchase from established, reputed stores
- Inflated auto/taxi fares: Insist on using the meter or app-based booking
- Unofficial temple guides charging high fees: Temples generally do not require paid guides for basic visits
- Overcharging at beachside stalls: Confirm prices before ordering food or renting items like horse rides

---

Travel Tips

- Best season: November to February
- What to pack: Light cottons for most of the year, an umbrella or raincoat during monsoon season (Oct-Dec)
- Local etiquette: Modest dress recommended at temples; some temples restrict entry for non-Hindus to inner sanctums
- Language: Tamil and English widely spoken
- Cash vs UPI: UPI widely accepted in shops and restaurants; cash useful for smaller vendors and autos
- Network availability: Good 4G/5G coverage across the city
- Medical facilities: Well-developed; Chennai is a major healthcare hub with multiple multi-specialty hospitals

Interesting Facts

1. Chennai was formerly known as Madras and was renamed in 1996.
2. Marina Beach is one of the longest urban beaches in the world, stretching approximately 13 km.
3. Fort St. George, built in 1644, was the first fortress built by the British in India.
4. Chennai is considered a major hub for Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam classical dance.
5. The Theosophical Society campus houses one of the largest banyan trees in Asia.
6. Chennai is a significant center for the automotive industry in India, sometimes referred to as the "Detroit of India."
7. Kapaleeshwarar Temple's current structure dates back to the 7th century, though the site's origins are believed to be older.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is 2 days enough for Chennai? Yes, though 3 days allows for a day trip to Mahabalipuram.
2. Is Chennai safe for solo travellers? Yes, it is generally considered safe with good public transport.
3. Which month is best to visit Chennai? November to February, for cooler and more comfortable weather.
4. What food should I try in Chennai? Idli-sambar, Chettinad cuisine, and filter coffee.
5. What should I avoid in Chennai? Overpriced "factory outlet" silk saree claims and unofficial temple guides.
6. Is Chennai good for a day trip to Mahabalipuram? Yes, it is approximately 60 km and a popular half-day to full-day excursion.
7. Is Chennai Metro tourist-friendly? Yes, it connects several key areas, though auto and taxi remain common for last-mile connectivity.
8. Is tap water safe to drink in Chennai? No, bottled or filtered water is recommended.
9. What is the best way to reach Chennai? Well connected by air, rail, and road from all major Indian cities.
10. Is Marina Beach safe for swimming? Caution is advised due to strong currents; many visitors prefer walking rather than swimming.
11. What is the dress code for temples in Chennai? Modest clothing is recommended; some temples restrict inner sanctum access to Hindus only.
12. Is haggling expected at markets? Yes, particularly in T Nagar and Pondy Bazaar for non-fixed-price items.
13. Is Chennai good for shopping? Yes, especially for Kanjeevaram silk sarees and traditional jewelry.
14. What is the best time to visit Marina Beach? Early morning or evening, avoiding the midday heat.
15. Is Chennai a good base to explore Tamil Nadu? Yes, it serves as a convenient gateway to Mahabalipuram, Kanchipuram, and Pondicherry.
