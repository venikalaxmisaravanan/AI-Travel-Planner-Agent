---

Short Introduction

Vellore is a historic city in Tamil Nadu, best known for the Vellore Fort, a 16th-century granite fortress built by Vijayanagara-era chieftains, and the Sri Lakshmi Narayani Golden Temple (Sripuram), a modern temple complex covered in gold. The city is also a major educational and medical hub, home to institutions like Christian Medical College (CMC) and Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), drawing significant numbers of students and medical visitors. Vellore is unique for combining historical fort architecture with its modern reputation as a center for education and healthcare tourism. It suits history enthusiasts, pilgrims visiting Sripuram, and travelers accompanying medical patients to CMC.

- Festivals: Local temple festivals at Sripuram, Pongal (Jan)

Top Tourist Attractions

Vellore Fort
Description: 16th-century granite fort with a moat, temple, mosque, and church within its walls, reflecting layered history
Time required: 1.5-2 hours
Entry fee: Free (small fees for specific sections/museum)
Best visiting time: Morning or late afternoon

Sri Lakshmi Narayani Golden Temple (Sripuram)
Description: Modern temple complex with a shrine covered in gold, surrounded by landscaped grounds
Time required: 1.5-2 hours
Entry fee: Free
Best visiting time: Morning or evening (avoid midday heat during the walk to the shrine)

Government Museum, Vellore Fort
Description: Museum within the fort complex showcasing regional historical artifacts
Time required: 45-60 minutes
Entry fee: Rs 10-20, approximate
Best visiting time: Morning to afternoon

Jalakandeswarar Temple
Description: Temple within Vellore Fort dedicated to Lord Shiva, known for its Vijayanagara-style architecture
Time required: 30-45 minutes
Entry fee: Free
Best visiting time: Morning

St. John's Church
Description: Colonial-era church located within Vellore Fort complex
Time required: 20-30 minutes
Entry fee: Free
Best visiting time: Morning

Safety Tips

- Areas to avoid: Isolated areas late at night away from the main city center
- Night travel advice: Use registered taxis or auto services after dark
- Emergency numbers: Police: 100, Ambulance: 108, Tourist Helpline: 1363
- General safety recommendations: Keep valuables secure in crowded bazaar areas; follow temple dress codes and etiquette at Sripuram

---

Common Tourist Scams

- Overpriced auto fares for CMC/VIT-area trips: Insist on using the meter or negotiate fares in advance
- Unofficial "guides" at Vellore Fort charging high fees: Basic visits typically do not require a paid guide
- Overcharging at souvenir stalls near Sripuram: Compare prices before purchasing
- Fake "priority darshan" claims at Sripuram: Entry is generally free and does not require paid priority access

Interesting Facts

1. Vellore Fort was built in the 16th century by Vijayanagara-era chieftains and later passed through Nawab, Maratha, and British control.
2. The Sripuram Golden Temple is reported to have used substantial quantities of gold in its construction, making it one of the most gold-covered structures in India.
3. Vellore Fort's premises uniquely house a Hindu temple, a mosque, and a church within the same complex.
4. Christian Medical College, Vellore, founded in 1900, is one of India's most renowned medical institutions, drawing patients from across the country and abroad.
5. Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), established in 1984, is a major private technical university with a large student population from across India and abroad.
6. Vellore Fort briefly held Tipu Sultan's family after the fall of Srirangapatna in the late 18th century.
7. The 1806 Vellore Mutiny, one of the earliest instances of Indian sepoy rebellion against British colonial rule, took place at Vellore Fort.
